LET'S CLEAR THINGS UP

Good questions.
Honest answers.

A little more about the app,
the stakes, and what comes next.

Okay, what exactly is Moltivate?+
Moltivate is a mobile fitness app we're building around team challenges. Friends agree on a workout goal, put a stake behind it, and hold each other accountable. Your accepted workouts contribute to your team's result.
Is this real money?+
This website is an interactive preview. The teams, progress, and SOL amounts are sample data; no money moves here. The first working app demo will use Solana devnet and test SOL. Actual-money features are not available yet.
How will my workout count?+
The planned app lets you record a supported exercise. Our verification service will analyze the recording and count accepted reps. The phone demo illustrates the experience; it doesn't access your camera or verify a real workout.
What if my team doesn't win?+
Our proposed rules return your stake when you complete your commitment, even if your team loses. Winning teammates who completed their goals share the forfeited stakes. Ties and settlement timeouts return everyone's stake. These rules are still being finalized and will be shown before any deposit.
Do I need to know anything about crypto?+
You don't need a wallet to explore this preview. For funded challenges in the planned app, you'll connect a supported Solana wallet and approve your own transactions. Moltivate will never ask for your seed phrase.
When can I get the app?+
We're building for iPhone and Android. There isn't an App Store or Google Play release yet, so the download buttons show “coming soon.” In the meantime, you can try the interactive phone preview above.
